The Morning Barrel

Thursday, October 1, 2026 — Houston

LNG Canada partners sanction Phase 2, doubling Kitimat export capacity and Montney drilling plans.

Crude sold off hard, with WTI down $3.21 to $96.16 and Brent off $6.01 to $113.96, as traders weighed rising non-OPEC supply against the Middle East shipping risks and Chinese fuel export uncertainty that had been supporting prices; crude stocks ticked up 922 kbbl. Henry Hub held near $3.18 as the injection season winds down with storage at 3,415 Bcf, up 64 Bcf and still tracking above the five-year average. ERCOT handled a 66 GW load today with solar supplying 28% of generation alongside gas at 43%, wind at 11%, and nuclear at 7%.

What matters today

  • LNG Canada's equity partners sanctioned a Phase 2 expansion on September 29 that will double the Kitimat, BC terminal's nameplate output, with no completion timeline but startup expected early next decade, triggering a parallel expansion of the Coastal GasLink pipeline and more Montney drilling to feed it.
  • Cheniere signed a long-term LNG supply agreement with Petrobras, adding Brazil as a term offtaker for US Gulf Coast volumes.
  • ExxonMobil awarded a subsea contract for its Rovuma LNG project in Mozambique, moving the long-delayed development forward.
  • CenterPoint Energy closed the $2.62 billion sale of its Ohio natural gas distribution business to National Fuel Gas Company, continuing its shift toward pure-play Texas/Indiana electric and gas operations.
  • Up to 11 Bcf/d of new Permian natural gas takeaway capacity is scheduled online through 2029, improving regional gas economics even as oil remains the primary driver of drilling activity there.
  • US motor gasoline inventories fell to a 12-year seasonal low, pushing the 3-2-1 crack spread to an all-time high and boosting refiner margins heading into fall turnaround season.

Threads worth following

  • Watch whether the Permian's expanding gas takeaway capacity starts pulling drilling decisions away from pure oil economics, since that would reshape associated-gas flaring and pipeline utilization for the rest of the decade.
  • The Senate's energy permitting bill could unlock the transmission buildout ERCOT needs to meet data-center-driven demand growth into the 2030s, a bottleneck that's been slowing both gas and renewable interconnections in Texas.
  • LNG Canada's Phase 2 FID puts Canadian Pacific Coast supply in direct competition with US Gulf Coast projects like Cheniere's expansions for Asian buyers, worth tracking as both regions race to add capacity this decade.
