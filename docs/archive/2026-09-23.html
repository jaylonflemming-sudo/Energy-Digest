What matters today

  • Governor Abbott broadened his pause on new Texas data center approvals to include environmental permits, halting activity statewide until December while ERCOT completes a grid capacity audit.
  • Williams agreed to buy Momentum Midstream for $5.5 billion, adding to a wave of midstream consolidation alongside Plains All American's $585 million purchase of Silver Creek's Powder River Basin gathering system.
  • TotalEnergies and Amni took final investment decision on the $1.108 billion Ima gas project in Nigeria, while PTTEP separately sanctioned a new offshore development in the Gulf of Thailand.
  • X-energy is advancing its first-of-a-kind small modular reactor design with a large infusion of federal funding aimed at restarting U.S. nuclear construction.
  • Activist investor Toms Capital sent Devon Energy a letter urging the company to explore a sale, adding it to the list of mid-cap E&Ps facing consolidation pressure.

Threads worth following

  • Texas's data center permitting freeze is the first real regulatory brake on the AI-driven load growth story and will shape how ERCOT plans capacity additions for the rest of the decade.
  • Midstream M&A (Williams-Momentum, Plains-Silver Creek) is redrawing basin gathering and takeaway economics in the Permian, DJ, and Powder River basins ahead of the next drilling cycle.
  • China's spare refining capacity could cap the global gasoline and diesel crunch that's been feeding into product cracks — worth watching whether Beijing actually ramps exports or holds back for domestic reasons.
