The Morning Barrel

Tuesday, September 8, 2026 — Houston

Williams closes $5.5B Momentum Midstream deal as crude jumps on supply concerns.

WTI jumped $4.45 to $91.48 and Brent $6.27 to $96.02, a sharp move that likely reflects a supply disruption or geopolitical escalation rather than a demand shift, given crude stocks drew down 4.45 million bbl on the week. Henry Hub held flat at $2.90 despite a 30 Bcf storage build to 3,214 Bcf, suggesting the gas market is shrugging off both the summer injection season and reports of softer LNG feedgas flows. ERCOT peak load hit 71,382 MW with solar the single largest fuel source at 39%, ahead of gas at 36%.

What matters today

  • Williams completed its $5.5 billion acquisition of Momentum Midstream, adding Gulf Coast gas gathering and processing assets to its portfolio.
  • IREN's 2 GW Sweetwater Hub in Texas won conditional ERCOT status as baseload in the grid operator's Batch Zero interconnection process, sending IREN shares up 7% and Applied Digital up 6% on the read-through for data-center power demand.
  • RBN Energy reports a new Permian gas pipeline is set to add outbound capacity that could flip the basin's multi-year takeaway-constraint story, easing the negative Waha pricing that has plagued producers.
  • A new Houston terminal opened to give Canadian crude a more direct path to global export markets, per the Alberta Energy Centre.
  • Solaris Energy Infrastructure raised its full-year 2026 guidance and issued first-quarter 2027 guidance, signaling continued demand for its power and fuel-management equipment.

Threads worth following

  • Watch whether the new Permian pipeline capacity RBN describes actually closes the Waha basis blowouts that have defined the basin for two years — if so, it changes the economics for associated-gas producers and processors alike.
  • ERCOT's Batch Zero conditional approvals for gigawatt-scale data center hubs like Sweetwater are a live test of how Texas plans to interconnect enormous new baseload demand without repeating past grid strain, especially with reports of 474 GW stuck in the broader interconnection queue.
  • Midstream consolidation keeps accelerating — Williams/Momentum follows a run of billion-dollar-plus deals — worth tracking who the next acquisition target is as gathering and processing assets get repriced around Permian and Haynesville gas growth.
