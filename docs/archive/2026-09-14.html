Williams buys Momentum Midstream for $5.5B as crude jumps $3+ on supply disruption fears

WTI and Brent both surged over $3 on the day, with Brent near $110, likely reflecting a geopolitical or supply-disruption trigger rather than a demand story given gas prices barely moved and crude stocks were roughly flat week-on-week. Henry Hub eased slightly to $2.81 as storage builds continue (3,254 Bcf, +40 Bcf) ahead of a mild-looking fall injection season. ERCOT's midday mix shows solar now the single largest fuel source at 34%, ahead of gas at 27%, underscoring how much daytime load Texas solar has absorbed this summer.

What matters today

  • Williams agreed to acquire Momentum Midstream for $5.5 billion, adding Gulf Coast gas gathering and processing assets to its network.
  • Baker Hughes expanded its equipment supply partnership with Venture Global, adding new LNG infrastructure orders tied to Gulf Coast export capacity growth.
  • ERCOT is surveying more than 400 data centers as part of Governor Abbott's audit of large-load interconnection requests on the Texas grid.
  • Soluna's Project Dorothy secured conditional ERCOT base-load status for 100 MW, part of 266 MW the company is working to preserve across two Texas campuses under new interconnection rules.
  • EPA moved to rescind existing federal limits on carbon pollution from power plants, a rule change with direct implications for coal and gas plant retirement timelines nationwide.

Threads worth following

  • Williams-Momentum deal signals continued consolidation in Gulf Coast gas gathering and processing as midstream operators position for rising LNG feedgas demand.
  • ERCOT's data-center audit and the Soluna/Sangha base-load classification fights are early tests of how Texas will ration grid capacity as large-load interconnection requests pile up.
  • EPA's rollback of power plant carbon limits, combined with DOE's ongoing orders keeping coal plants open, points to a federal push to slow retirements even as solar keeps taking daytime market share in ERCOT.
