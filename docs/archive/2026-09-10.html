What matters today

  • Enbridge agreed to buy another crude oil pipeline system for $2.55 billion just as Greg Ebel prepares to hand the CEO role to Colin Harradence.
  • Core Scientific won conditional ERCOT approval for 1 GW of capacity across its Texas sites, adding to the state's data-center power queue.
  • Williams closed its acquisition of Momentum Midstream, expanding its Gulf Coast gathering and processing footprint.
  • Eni Cyprus selected McDermott to execute the offshore Cronos gas development, advancing the Eastern Mediterranean project toward construction.
  • Gulf Coast Midstream launched an open season for its Nash gas storage project, seeking shipper commitments as Gulf Coast LNG demand grows.
  • Ohio regulators unanimously approved 149 MW of solar plus 149 MW of battery storage at the former Hamden coal site, a rare clean-energy approval in the state.
