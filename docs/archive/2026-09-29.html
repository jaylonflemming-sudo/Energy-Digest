Fervo Energy's Cape Station in Utah becomes first utility-scale next-gen geothermal plant online.

What matters today

  • Fervo Energy began selling power to the grid from its Cape Station enhanced geothermal project in Utah, the first utility-scale next-gen geothermal facility to reach commercial operation.
  • TotalEnergies took final investment decision on full-field development of the Absheron gas field offshore Azerbaijan.
  • Argent LNG awarded a FEED contract for its proposed liquefaction plant at Port Fourchon, Louisiana, advancing the project toward FID.
  • Comstock Resources signed a $1.65 billion framework deal with Azerbaijan's Socar covering Haynesville shale gas development.
  • Cheniere Energy and Petrobras signed a long-term LNG sale and purchase agreement, and USD Group agreed to sell its Port Arthur crude terminal to Jefferson Energy for $255 million.

Threads worth following

  • Gulf Coast gas storage capacity is falling behind LNG export growth, meaning new export terminals may increasingly compete for scarce, costlier storage — watch which projects secure capacity contracts first.
  • Permian gas output is at record highs thanks to Blackcomb, Hugh Brinson, and the Gulf Coast Express expansion, easing associated-gas bottlenecks that have plagued Permian oil producers.
  • ERCOT's data-center-driven demand growth (ENGIE's 568 MW Oracle supply deal is one signal) is forcing utilities and generators to lock in long-term power contracts years ahead of expected 2030s peak load.
