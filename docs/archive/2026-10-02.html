LNG Canada Phase 2 FID locks in major Montney drilling and pipeline expansion

What matters today

  • LNG Canada's Phase 2 FID is driving a parallel expansion of the Coastal GasLink pipeline and a pickup in Montney drilling activity to feed it, per RBN Energy.
  • Esentia Energy's new gas pipeline deal creates a continuous Permian-to-Pacific-Coast-of-Mexico link, drawing attention to the previously low-profile Gato Negro LNG project.
  • Ksi Lisims LNG signed a 20-year, 1 MMtpa heads-of-agreement with Centrica, the latest offtake deal for the British Columbia export project.
  • YPF, Eni, and XRG are targeting a November FID for Argentina LNG, a project aimed at monetizing Vaca Muerta gas for export.
  • INEOS started commercial-scale CO2 injection at Project Greensand offshore Denmark, one of the first operating CCS projects to move gas-field depleted reservoirs into service as storage.
  • California Gov. Newsom signed legislation formally enabling virtual power plants, after previously being expected to veto similar measures.

Threads worth following

  • Permian associated gas hit a record in July and is now the swing supply behind both new Gulf Coast/Mexico pipeline capacity and Western Canada LNG feedgas — watch for basis and takeaway bottlenecks as multiple projects compete for the same molecules.
  • Gasoline inventories at a 12-year low with record 3-2-1 crack spreads signal tight refining capacity heading into fall turnarounds — a margin story that could reassert itself at the pump even as crude prices soften.
  • ERCOT's battery buildout is reportedly cycling between boom and bust on economics even as forecasters flag a load surge this decade from data centers — the mismatch between near-term BESS revenue and long-term demand growth is a live policy and investment question in Texas.
