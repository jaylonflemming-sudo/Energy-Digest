What matters today

  • Cheniere completed Corpus Christi Liquefaction Stage 3 on August 28, taking custody of the seventh and final train and making the Texas facility the second-largest LNG export plant in the US.
  • Enbridge is acquiring Tallgrass Energy's crude oil transportation business for $2.55 billion, including a 75% stake in the 1,050-mile Pony Express Pipeline linking Rockies production to Cushing.
  • The gas processing frac spread hit $6.15/MMBtu last week, its highest level since February 2024, sharpening the economics of stripping NGLs from raw gas.
  • ERCOT is surveying more than 400 data centers as part of Governor Abbott's grid audit while Soluna secured conditional Base Load status for its 100 MW Dorothy campus and a Houston-area battery project came online in six weeks amid record demand.
  • Hitachi Energy is putting $528 million into Mississippi, its largest US investment to date, creating 654 jobs to support grid equipment manufacturing.

Threads worth following

  • The Saudi pipeline shutdown is worth tracking for duration and volume lost — a prolonged outage would tighten global sour crude balances just as US refiners are already running near capacity.
  • ERCOT's data center survey and the wave of conditional Base Load approvals (Soluna, Sangha) signal Texas regulators are trying to get ahead of load growth before it strains summer peaks again.
  • Corpus Christi Stage 3's completion adds to a broader US LNG capacity buildout that's colliding with new demand-side deals (China's expanded supply agreement, Chevron's Argentina and Mediterranean push, PTT's Gulf Coast search) — worth watching how contracted volumes get allocated as more trains start up.
